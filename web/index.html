Badminton video analysis

Upload a video of you playing. The AI will give an overall assessment of your play style, suggest suitable rackets and ways to improve.

Drag and drop a video here or click to choose

MP4, MOV, WebM · max 200 MB · ideally film the whole court, 30 seconds to 3 minutes

Tips for a better AI assessment

  • Film from behind or from a high angle so the whole court is visible.
  • Use good lighting, keep the camera steady, and include both attacking and defensive rallies.
  • Mention your skill level and the racket you use when you chat with the AI.
Badminton Doctor Suggestions are for reference only. Try the racket in person if you can.
